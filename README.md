# Paysense-

Patience of your customers is in our hands.


## About Paysense

Paysense is an automated payment recovery system built for online businesses. When people shop online, their payments often stop before finishing. This happens because of small everyday issues like typing a wrong UPI PIN on a phone, a bank computer taking too long to reply, a weak mobile network, or not having enough money in the bank at that moment.

When a payment drops, most websites show an error screen and do nothing else. The shopper feels confused or worried, and the business loses the sale.

Paysense steps in right away behind the scenes. It figures out the real reason why the payment stopped and handles it carefully. It does not send annoying messages to buyers. Instead, it waits for the right moment and uses the right method to help the buyer finish their purchase calmly.


## Sectors and Companies Paysense Can Help

Paysense is useful for any business that accepts digital payments online. It directly helps the following industries:

1. Online Shopping Websites
Stores that sell clothes, electronics, shoes, and home goods face high cart drops. Many shoppers want the item but face a payment glitch. Paysense helps them complete the order before they leave for another website.

2. Quick Delivery and Grocery Apps
Apps that deliver groceries, food, and daily essentials within minutes need instant payments. When a bank times out during peak dinner or morning hours, Paysense can retry the bank quietly so the order goes through without making the customer order again.

3. Travel and Ticket Booking Companies
Websites that sell train tickets, flight seats, bus seats, and hotel rooms deal with urgent payments. If a payment drops while booking, the customer panics about losing their seat. Paysense helps recover the booking quickly.

4. Subscription Services and Software Providers
Companies that offer monthly software tools, streaming services, and online memberships lose customers when monthly card debits get declined. Paysense sends helpful emails with alternate ways to pay so users keep their service active.

5. Education and Online Courses
Platforms selling coaching classes, college preparation courses, and certification tests sell high value courses. When a payment stops, students or parents might hesitate to try again. Paysense provides a gentle reminder with a reserved seat link.

6. Healthcare and Online Doctor Apps
Platforms where people book doctor calls, buy medicines, or schedule lab tests need calm and reliable payments. When someone is unwell, a failed payment causes stress. Paysense handles the recovery smoothly.


## Who Benefits from Paysense

Paysense helps multiple groups within a business as well as the end customers:

1. Business Owners and Finance Managers
They protect their revenue. Instead of losing sales that were already at the checkout step, they recover that money without spending extra on advertising.

2. Marketing and Growth Teams
Marketing teams spend a lot of money to bring visitors to a website. When a visitor reaches checkout and the payment stops, that marketing money is wasted. Paysense makes sure those visitors actually turn into paying customers.

3. Customer Support Teams
Support teams spend hours answering messages from people asking whether their money was cut or if their order was placed. Paysense prevents these support tickets by keeping the customer informed or retrying quietly.

4. Normal Online Shoppers
Everyday customers get a much better shopping experience. They do not have to worry about whether their card was charged twice, and they do not have to fill in their address and cart details all over again.


## How Paysense Works in Real Situations

Paysense checks why a transaction stopped and picks the best path:

1. When a customer enters the wrong UPI PIN
The shopper made a simple typing mistake. Paysense waits eight minutes so the person has time to open their bank app and check their balance. Then it sends a friendly WhatsApp message with a direct link to finish the checkout.

2. When a bank server takes too long to reply
This is a bank issue, not the customer fault. Paysense does not bother the customer at all. It waits five minutes for the bank computers to clear their queue and then quietly retries the payment behind the scenes.

3. When the internet connection drops
The shopper was in an elevator, traveling, or lost signal for a moment. Paysense waits twelve minutes and sends a short push notification to their phone so they can complete the order with one tap.

4. When there is not enough money in the bank account
Pushing the customer right away feels disrespectful. Paysense waits until the next morning between 9 AM and 11 AM, when people often transfer money or check their accounts, and sends a polite WhatsApp note letting them know their items are still saved.

5. When a card is declined
Cards get declined for spending limits, fraud flags, or disabled online permissions. Paysense waits two hours and sends a helpful email explaining how to finish the payment with another card or another payment method.

6. When a payment method is not supported
If the checkout does not support a specific payment type, Paysense immediately notifies the merchant store team so they can fix their settings. It never sends a confusing message to the buyer.


## Rules That Protect the Customer

Paysense has strict built in safety rules so it never annoys people:

1. No spam limit: Paysense will never attempt more than three recovery actions for any payment.
2. Time limit: Paysense stops completely if three days pass since the payment attempt.
3. Respectful language: Messages sent to customers never use harsh words like error, declined, rejected, or failed. The tone is always helpful and polite.


## How to Run Paysense on Your Computer

1. Open a terminal in the paysense folder.
2. Create and activate a Python virtual environment.
3. Install the required Python packages from requirements.txt.
4. Build the web dashboard inside the client folder using npm run build.
5. Start the server by running python start.py.
6. Open your web browser and visit http://127.0.0.1:5000 to see the live system and dashboard.
