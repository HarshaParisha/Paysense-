## Project Summary

Every day across India, millions of online shoppers abandon their carts because of momentary digital payment disruptions. Whether it is an incorrect UPI MPIN keyed in on a smartphone, a momentary bank gateway timeout, a sudden Wi-Fi dropout, or temporary insufficient balance before payday, standard payment gateways respond with a sterile, alarming failure notification. This abrupt friction shatters buyer confidence, leaving customers feeling anxious or annoyed and forcing merchants to lose hard-earned sales. For small and mid-sized Razorpay merchants, cart abandonment caused by payment friction represents tens of thousands of rupees in lost revenue each week.

PaySense solves this breakdown by acting as an autonomous payment failure recovery agent that protects both merchant revenue and customer trust. Powered by intelligent classification and an empathetic decision framework, PaySense intercepts raw Razorpay webhook events in real time. Instead of bombarding the customer with immediate, generic alerts, PaySense diagnoses the precise root cause and orchestrates customized recovery paths. It performs silent gateway retries for bank timeouts, dispatches gentle WhatsApp reminders for UPI pin slips, schedules next-morning notifications during salary windows for insufficient funds, sends helpful card resolution emails, and immediately escalates unsupported payment methods to the merchant without bothering the customer. Strict customer protection rules ensure no customer receives more than three attempts or is contacted outside a 72-hour window.

In our synthetic benchmark of 50 authentic Indian payment failure events across all six failure categories, PaySense demonstrated remarkable performance. The system recovered 30 out of 48 eligible attempts, achieving an overall recovery rate of 62.5% and reclaiming ₹2,25,204 in gross merchandise value that would otherwise have been abandoned. High-intent friction points responded exceptionally well, with UPI PIN errors recovering at 77.8% and bank timeouts recovering at 84.6% via silent gateway re-attempts. PaySense proves that with timely, respectful, and intelligent recovery, merchant patience and customer loyalty go hand in hand.
